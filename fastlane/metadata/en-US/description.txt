SimplePenNote — pen and paper, nothing more.

Capture a thought, jot a quick memo, or make a simple sketch. One tap creates a note, your last pen color and width are remembered, and the collapsible toolbar makes room to write.

-- Six paper templates
Blank, grid, lined, Cornell notes, weekly planner, and study (problem/answer). Start with blank paper, or choose a template when you need it. Your last choice is remembered.

-- Spacious zoomable canvas
Built on PencilKit. Pinch to zoom freely; one page holds plenty.

-- Face ID / Touch ID app lock
Keep journals and private notes safe. One toggle in settings.

-- 30-day trash
Accidentally deleted notes are recoverable for 30 days.

-- Silent iCloud sync
Notes sync across your iPhone and iPad signed in with the same Apple ID. No third-party servers — notes live only in your private iCloud.

-- PNG / PDF export
Preview PNG images and automatically crop unused space around your handwriting for free. PDFs preserve the whole paper. Free exports include a watermark.

-- Find and reuse notes
Search by title, duplicate notes, and choose pen colors and widths.
