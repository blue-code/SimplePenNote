SimplePenNote is a minimal handwriting notebook. Notes for review:

1. No account or sign-in. All notes are stored only in the user's private iCloud (CloudKit private database).
2. In-app purchase: SimplePenNote Pro (one-time, non-consumable). The free version includes 5 pages, every paper template, and PNG/PDF export with a watermark. The paywall appears at the 5-page limit, from Settings, and from Pro-only actions (watermark-free export, transparent PNG, Combine into PDF, Handwriting Library).
3. To try the Handwriting Library: open a note, tap the dashed-rectangle button at the top, drag around some handwriting, tap Preview, then Save to Handwriting Library (Pro). Open My Notes > Handwriting Library to insert it into another note.
4. Face ID / Touch ID is used only if the user turns on App Lock in Settings, and only the authentication result is used.
5. No third-party servers, analytics SDKs, or ads.
6. The app is localized in Korean, English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese.
